# Judge-facing shopping copy

Owner decision, October 25, 2026 (New York): use normal shopping language and consolidate repeated demo/simulated notices. This replaces earlier presentation requirements to repeat those labels in headings, seller lines, price lines and stock labels. It does not change any catalog record or payment setting.

The homepage and guided results retain a visible no-money/no-shipment evaluation notice, with reference-price/stock and nonpartner disclosures. The adapter recommendation places its notice in the visible footer, outside expandable details. Cable requirements remain on the product card. A missing or unavailable item is still labelled unavailable rather than represented as buyable.

The local mock panel says Local payment preview and explicitly states it does not contact PayPal or charge money. PayPal Sandbox headings, routes and verification messages are unchanged and remain exclusive to that mode. Technical state and evidence labels remain available. Checkout confirmations still say that confirmation does not take payment; existing tax, shipping, quote, expiry and permission disclosures are retained.

Recommendations/Recommended replace Suggestions/Suggested in English; Spanish uses Recomendaciones/Recomendado. No AI provider/source attribution is removed. No public-store or live-inventory claim is introduced. Product-photo reuse limitations remain open in product-photo-sources.md.

## Approved plain-language pass

The owner approved simplifying common customer wording, with the same decisions and safeguards. Main examples:

| Before | After |
| --- | --- |
| Show laptops and assessments | Find my laptop |
| Review the understood need | Check your choices |
| Confirm this use and budget | Confirm my choices |
| Reply or correction | Your reply |
| Your confirmed brief | Your choices |
| Ready with caveats | May fit — check details |
| Review and confirm this purchase summary | Check your purchase |
| Confirm this purchase summary | Confirm purchase details |
| Create a purchase permission | Set purchase limits |
| Create permission | Save purchase permission |

Spanish text is updated alongside English. Recommended items have a green text badge; the badge is noninteractive, carries its meaning in words, and follows the existing recommended-variant selection. Other eligible items remain Another option. The eligible adapter recommendation has the same badge. These are presentation labels, not a claim that every product meets every need. Orange primary shopping buttons keep navy text and visible focus; disabled, cancel and revoke controls keep their existing behavior/styles. Raw identifiers, amounts, confirmation fields and financial state transitions do not change.
