# MacBook Pro 16-inch 2019 charging guide

Checked October 27, 2026. Information and external handover only; not an expanded checkout catalog.

## Official evidence

- https://support.apple.com/en-us/111932 — Apple lists the 16-inch 2019 model with USB-C charging ports, a 96W USB-C adapter and a USB-C charge cable in the box. The specification includes an Intel Core i7 configuration.
- https://support.apple.com/en-us/109509 — Apple recommends a 96W USB-C adapter with a USB-C charge cable for the 16-inch 2019 MacBook Pro. Lower-wattage USB PD charging is not declared incompatible; Apple recommends meeting its stated wattage for the best charging experience.
- https://www.apple.com/shop/product/mw2l3am/a/96w-usb-c-power-adapter — US product page lists USD79, cable sold separately, and MacBook Pro 16-inch 2019 in compatibility. This dated reference is not a server quote or a stock guarantee.
- https://support.apple.com/en-us/108052 — Apple identifies this family as MacBookPro16,1 and MacBookPro16,4. Matching the family for informational guidance does not assign one of these identifiers to a shopper's stored device.
